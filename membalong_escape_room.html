🧩 Your Mission

Today, you and your team are chosen as the New Guardians.

🏔️ Level 1 - The Majestic Rock: Explore the sacred granite formations of Batu Baginde.

Each mission gives you a piece of a Secret Code. Collect all digits to unlock the chest and claim your title as a Global Ambassador.

Rules of the Game

Know before you play!

  • ✓ Read Carefully: The context text holds the answers.
  • 📊 Pass Requirement: You must score at least 80% correct to pass a level.
  • ❤️ 7 Lives: You have 7 lives for all levels. Wrong answers cost 1 life.
  • ⭐ Scoring: Correct answers give 2 pts (1 pt for Level 9). Max 10 pts per level!
  • ✨ Ask Ancestors: Stuck? Use the Gemini AI button to ask the Ancestral Spirits for a hint!
  • 🔐 Bonus Codes: Complete a level successfully to receive one digit of the final vault code.
